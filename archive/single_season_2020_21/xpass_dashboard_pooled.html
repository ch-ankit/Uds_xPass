Can We Predict Every Pass?
Building an xPass Model for Football

XGBoost trained on StatsBomb open event data across 16 La Liga seasons (2004/05–2020/21). We ask whether weak foot hurts completion, how calibration varies by pass type, and which players consistently beat difficulty-adjusted expectations.

04 — CALIBRATION

Where does the model fail?

ECE = Expected Calibration Error. Lower is better. Model is near-perfect on ordinary passes but degrades sharply on rare types.
Root cause confirmed Isotonic recalibration halves ECE on through balls (n=554) but worsens it on crosses (n=515). Success and failure scale with sample size — directly confirming data scarcity, not missing features, as the cause.

Methodology: XGBoost trained on 17 event-data features, match-level 80/20 split. Preferred foot inferred from seasonal pass foot-usage (>55% threshold, min. 20 passes). Position z-scores normalised within GK / DEF / MID / ATT. 16 La Liga seasons available from StatsBomb open data (season 2015/16 excluded — 380 matches, processed separately). AUC 0.85–0.90 per season. Full calibration analysis pools 18 seasons including partial releases.
